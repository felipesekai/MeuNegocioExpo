# Logo Generation Prompt

**Context:**
"MeuNegocioExpo" is a mobile app designed to help small business owners manage their daily operations, including clients, products, and orders. It aims to be simple, efficient, and work offline.

**Prompt:**
> Create a modern, minimalist app icon design for a business management application called "Meu Negócio". The design should convey organization, efficiency, and growth.
>
> **Visual Elements:**
> *   Consider incorporating abstract symbols representing inventory (like a stylized box or cube), growth (an upward arrow or graph line), or commerce (a tag or bag).
> *   Avoid clutter; keep the lines clean and bold.
>
> **Style:**
> *   Flat design or soft neumorphism.
> *   Vector art style.
> *   Rounded corners suitable for an iOS/Android app icon.
>
> **Colors:**
> *   Primary: Trustworthy Blue (e.g., #0056b3) or Growth Green (e.g., #28a745).
> *   Accent: A touch of vibrant Orange (e.g., #fd7e14) or White for contrast.
> *   Background: Clean solid color or a subtle gradient.
>
> **Vibe:**
> *   Professional yet accessible.
> *   Reliable and modern.
